# Luckgrid

> **Multiply the power of your workforce.**

Luckgrid is building a network of independently operated systems that connect
people, agents, tools, knowledge, workflows, and operations through explicit
interfaces.

Our public GitHub organization profile lives in
[`profile/README.md`](profile/README.md). Its light- and dark-mode artwork is
maintained in [`profile/assets/`](profile/assets/).

## Explore

- [Luckgrid](https://www.luckgrid.net/) — the company, its approach, and ways to
  work together.
- [Knowledge](https://www.luckgrid.net/posts/) — public notes, use cases, and
  working proof.
- [Public repositories](https://github.com/orgs/luckgrid/repositories) — source,
  prototypes, starter workspaces, and systems under active development.
- [Map your system](mailto:admin@luckgrid.net?subject=Map%20Your%20System) —
  start with the product, workflow, integration, or operation you need to
  improve.

## What stays consistent

1. Lead with workforce capability and the outcomes people actually need.
2. Keep Architecture, Design, and Engineering visible as the practical path.
3. Describe independence, explicit interfaces, responsible operation, and
   evidence without making unsupported security or availability claims.
4. Link only to public websites and repositories; project maturity belongs to
   its own source of truth.
5. Keep the organization profile accessible, readable, and useful in both light
   and dark mode.
6. Present languages, frameworks, clouds, and tools as replaceable examples,
   never as a prescribed stack or the limit of Luckgrid's capabilities.
